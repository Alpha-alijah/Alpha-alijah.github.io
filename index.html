About

I'm a CUNY Hunter College graduate with a B.A. in Geography, a minor in Computer Science, and a Spatial Data Science Certificate. I work with spatial and multi-source data: collecting it, checking it for accuracy, analyzing it, and turning it into clear maps, dashboards, and recommendations.

Before and alongside my research, I worked in logistics as a dispatcher and delivery associate across Manhattan, which gave me a street-level understanding of how New York moves. I'm especially interested in transit, city planning, and public-sector data work.

NSF REU Research, CUNY CREST · Jun 2024 – May 2025
